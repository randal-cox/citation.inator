Empathy sounds like a natural antidote to political hostility. Yet empathic concern can also deepen partisan polarization (Simas et al., 2020). Open the citation to compare the journal version with the author’s preprint.

Feiler (2026) writes about gatherings as occasions for social connection. An ethnography of secular communities gives a different view of how shared practices acquire meaning (Frost, 2020). The first source is a hardcover edition. The second leads to a university repository PDF.

A reading list can hold several kinds of evidence side by side (Simas et al., 2020; Feinberg & Willer, 2019). It can also preserve a reference still under review until an editor verifies it.

Try a page locator on Molly Farneth’s print edition (Farneth, 2023, 34). Open the citation to see two library services for the print edition.

Related reading can share the same parentheses (Farneth, 2023; see also Feiler, 2026). The lettered links in Farneth’s endnote return to either mention.
