# Milestone 0.6.0 — Connected bibliographies and source maintenance

**In development; first adapter preview, no final release or connected-editor acceptance.** Randal authorized starting 0.6 on 25 November, then requested and completed a detailed plan/fresh-agent discussion. He endorsed the modest loop, accepted saved-work offline scope and authorized bounded connection experiments with his running Zotero/BEST library. Version 0.5.1 is released and adopted by draft. The [reviewed Zotero plan](../plans/zotero-integration.md) is the current design proposal, not a shipped integration or final 0.6 scope change.

## Agreed scope

- Ingest bibliography-manager interchange files, initially BibTeX, RIS and CSL-JSON.
- Explore explicit, read-only connections to Zotero and other managers for selected items or collections.
- Preserve identifiers, provenance, edition distinctions and local edits; review proposed reimport changes rather than replacing records silently.
- Review broken or stale source links, keeping failed checks distinct from evidence that a work is unavailable.
- Improve sourced correction, retraction and expression-of-concern notices. Verify that the notice applies to the cited work and retain the original citation and editorial decision.
- Reuse the reviewed-import records and decision model instead of creating a second identity system.

Embedded Zotero metadata encountered during article extraction remains a 0.4.0 preservation concern. External collections, manager services and ongoing source upkeep belong here.

Test permissions, origin behavior, disconnected/unsynced states and duplicate identity before choosing transport/failover. No automatic bidirectional sync is agreed.

Keep Zotero base metadata and document-specific assertions distinguishable so neither silently overwrites the other.
