# Milestone 0.4.0 — Reviewed citation import

**In development on a separate branch; excluded from 0.3.0.** The maintainer confirmed the separation on 21 November 2026.

## Delivered prototype

Recognition/matching, partial reference rules, source maps and a document-first local review demo exist on `codex/citation-import`. They are experimental and are not included as installed APIs or demo services in this stable-integration candidate. See the [development protocol and evidence](https://github.com/randal-cox/citation.inator/blob/codex/citation-import/docs/import-development.md).

## Acceptance and evidence

Import has its own synthetic/private development corpus and bounded reviews. Those are not acceptance for native files, general field accuracy, installed APIs or release.

## Known limits and follow-up

Complete native document adapters, measured extraction/field parsing, review/editor transactions, APIs/CLI/docs and adopter checks. Comprehensive learning follows in [0.5.0](0.5.0.md); bibliography connections in [0.6.0](0.6.0.md).
